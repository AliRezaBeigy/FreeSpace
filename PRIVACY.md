# Privacy Policy for Free Space

**Last updated:** November 23, 2026

**Free Space** ("the App") is designed with privacy as a core principle.

## 1. Data Collection
The App **does not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage data, or analytics.

## 2. Permissions
- **No Internet Access:** The App does not request the `INTERNET` permission. It cannot send data to a remote server.
- **Media access (opt-in):** The App may request media read access (`READ_MEDIA_VIDEO` / `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, or legacy storage read on older Android) so it can list large photos and videos, estimate savings, and compress items you select. Scanning runs only after you grant access. Replacing an original always requires your confirmation.

## 3. Third-Party Services
The App contains **no** third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising SDKs.

## 4. Local Processing
All photo and video compression is performed locally on your device using Android media APIs and hardware acceleration where available. Your media never leaves your device.

## 5. Contact
Open an issue on this project's GitHub repository when it is published.
